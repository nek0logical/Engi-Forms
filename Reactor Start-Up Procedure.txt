This document serves to promote proper engineering protocol and eliminate unsafe working practices. It is generalised for most NanoTrasen nuclear reactor installations, and engineers are encouraged to note station specific infrastructure.

[head=3]Pre-Startup Checklist[/head]
1. Pre-Startup Reactor Inspection Checklist (ENG-N00) has been completed [check]

[bold]Pressurised Gas Mix[/bold]
1. The coolant gas mix to be used in the reactor has been discussed with and approved by the Chief Engineer [check]

2. The moderative gas has been injected into the [bold][color=#018260]feedwater gas pipenet[/bold][/color] [check]

If using a plasma-based mix:
3. Plasma has been injected into the  [bold][color=#018260]feedwater gas pipenet[/bold][/color] to the approved composition threshold [check]

4. An appropriate system has been constructed to filter out and capture Tritium safely [check]

[bold]Reactor Chamber Design[/bold]
1. The material and configuration of rods within the reactor chamber has been discussed with and approved by the Chief Engineer [check]

With the exception of fuel rods:
2a. All rods have been manufactured and inserted into the reactor chamber in the approved configuration [check]

[bold]OR[/bold], in the case of insufficient material surplus:
2b. A functional and safe [italic]in-progress[/italic] configuration has been constructed until materials are sufficient to manufacture all rods for the final approved configuration [check]

[bold]Gas Turbine Operation[/bold]
1. The Flow Rate of the gas turbine is set to the optimal value (100 * number of gas channels + 200) [check]

If using an automatic controller:
2. At least one OR gate is configured to raise the Stator Load when the gas turbine reaches high speeds [check]

3. At least one OR gate is configured to lower the Stator Load when the gas turbine reaches low speeds [check]

[head=3]Reactor Startup Procedure[/head]
1. Ensure the control rods have been inserted to 100% [check]

2. Announce that the reactor has been started, and establish a radioactive exclusion zone around the nuclear reactor [check]

3. Insert the approved fuel rods into their designated slots within the reactor chamber [check]

4. Withdraw the control rods to 40% [check]

5. Monitor gas turbine RPM, and adjust stator load manually if required to prevent stalling and overspeed [check]

6. Completely withdraw control rods, monitoring the reaction to ensure stability [check]

7. At least five minutes have lapsed and both the nuclear reactor and gas turbine are stable [check]
